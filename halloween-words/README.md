# Halloween Words

A listening game: a voice says a Halloween word and the child taps the matching character.

**Words:** ghost, bat, pumpkin, witch, cat, skeleton, monster, spider

## How to play

1. **Learn:** the first screen shows all the characters with their names. Tap one to hear it.
2. **Play (▶):** the voice says a word, which is also written in the bubble at the top. Tap the right character.
   - Tap the orange 🔊 button to hear the word again.
   - A wrong character just wobbles and the word is said again. After two wrong taps, or a long pause, the right character glows.
3. Each round asks every word twice (16 questions), in random order, never the same word twice in a row.
   The characters change places after every question, so the child has to listen, not remember positions.

The characters are drawn as SVG in `characters.js` (friendly, not scary). Words are spoken with the browser's speech synthesis (British English voice when available).
