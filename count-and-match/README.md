# Count and Match

Teaches how the numbers 1 to 10 are written in English by connecting each number word to a card with that many things.

**Words:** one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten

## How to play

1. Press ▶ to start. The start screen shows a small animated example of a connection.
2. The board has number **words** on the left and **cards with things to count** on the right (🍎🍎🍎).
3. Connect a word to the card with the same number of things:
   - **drag** a line from one card to the other, or
   - **tap** one card, then tap its partner.
   Tapping a word says it aloud, and tapping a card of things makes them hop one by one, like counting.
4. A right match draws a coloured line and says the word. A wrong match just wobbles, and the word is said again.
   After two wrong tries for the same word, or a long pause, the right pair glows.
5. Each round covers all 10 numbers in random order, on 2 boards of 5, and ends with a celebration.

Words are spoken with the browser's speech synthesis (British English voice when available).
