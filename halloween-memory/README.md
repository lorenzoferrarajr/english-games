# Halloween Memory

A listening and memory game with the Halloween characters.

**Words:** ghost, bat, pumpkin, witch, cat, skeleton, monster, spider

## How to play

1. All the cards start face up. Tap a card to hear its name.
2. Press ▶: the cards flip face down and a few of them swap places.
3. The voice says a word (also written in the bubble). Tap the card you think it is; it flips over.
   - **Right card:** celebration, and the card disappears.
   - **Wrong card:** it shows itself and says its name, then flips back.
   - Either way, the next turn asks for a **different** card (unless only one card is left).
   - No hints: cards never glow or give themselves away. After a pause with no tap, the word is just said again.
   - Tap the orange 🔊 button to hear the word again.
4. The round ends when all cards are found. Then press ▶ to see them face up again and play another round.

Settings at the top of `game.js`: `SWAPS` (how many swaps after flipping) and timings.

The characters come from `../shared/halloween/characters.js`, shared with Halloween Words.
